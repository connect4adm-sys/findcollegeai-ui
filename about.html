Clarifications

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our data integrity, tools, and counseling.

FindCollege.ai brings together AI-powered guidance, institution discovery, psychometric assessments and education resources to help students make more informed decisions. Specific recommendations should be checked against current official institution information.

Availability and pricing may vary by tool. Check the relevant product page for current access details before starting an assessment or using a feature.

Use official sources and compare factors such as fees, eligibility, location, courses and outcomes. Rankings and claims should be reviewed against their source, date and methodology; no single metric should decide a student's future.

Compare course curriculum, recognition and accreditation, total cost, location, eligibility, student support, internships and placement methodology. Confirm important details on official college and regulator websites.

Parents can use institution information as a starting point to compare published fees and other factors. Always confirm the latest fee schedule, hostel costs, refund rules and scholarships directly with the institution.
